## TOP 5 KEY CONCEPTS

### C1: Letter-Numbers & Algebraic Expressions (Section 4.1)
- Letters represent unknown/variable numbers: a for Aftab's age, n for count, c for cost
- Algebraic expression: a mathematical expression with letter-numbers (a + 3, 2n, c×35 + j×60)
- Formulas: concise algebraic expressions for general relationships (perimeter of square = 4q)
- Same situation described in words → short formula (Shabnam's age = Aftab's age + 3 → s = a + 3)
**Sim:** Expression builder from word descriptions

### C2: Omitting the × Symbol & Reading Expressions (Section 4.3)
- 4 × n is shortened to 4n; 5 × m + 3 = 5m + 3
- Critical distinctions: 5u ≠ 5 + u (5 times u vs 5 more than u)
- 10y − 3 ≠ 10(y − 3) (3 less than 10 times y vs 10 times (y minus 3))
- Number sequence patterns: nth term of 4,8,12,16... is 4n
**Sim:** "Are they equal?" quiz — compare two expressions for different values of the variable

### C3: Simplification — Like and Unlike Terms (Section 4.4)
- Like terms share the same letter-numbers: 5c, 3c, 10c are all like terms → combine to 18c
- Unlike terms can't be combined: 18c + 11d stays as is
- Distributive property: 5c + 3c + 10c = (5+3+10)c = 18c
- Opening brackets: (40x+75y)−(6x+10y) = 40x+75y−6x−10y = 34x+65y
- Perimeter: l+b+l+b = 2l+2b
**Sim:** Drag-and-combine like terms; step-by-step simplification viewer

### C4: Evaluating Expressions — Mind the Mistakes (Section 4.1-4.3)
- Replace letter-numbers with numbers and compute
- Common mistakes: 3d with d=6 → should be 18, NOT 36; 10−a with a=−4 → should be 14, NOT 6
- 9 total "Mind the Mistake" problems catching typical student errors
**Sim:** "Spot the Error" quiz — find and correct mistakes in evaluation

### C5: Patterns & Formulas with Algebra (Section 4.5)
- Number machines: find formula from input→output pairs (2a−b)
- Matchstick patterns: y triangles need 2y+1 sticks; also 3+2(y−1)
- Calendar patterns: 2×2 square diagonal sums = 2a+8 (always equal!)
- Repeating design patterns: nth occurrence of Design B at position 3n−1
- 4-column grid: number at row r, column c = 4(r−1)+c
**Sim:** Pattern explorer + formula detective (find the rule)
