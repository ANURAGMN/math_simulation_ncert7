Triangle Inequality

When can three lengths form a triangle?

✅
Triangle CAN be formed!
Try these examples:

📝 Triangle Inequality Check

4 + 5 = 9 > 6 ✓
5 + 6 = 11 > 4 ✓
4 + 6 = 10 > 5 ✓

📐 The Rule

For any triangle, the sum of any two sides must be greater than the third side. If even one of the three checks fails, a triangle cannot be formed!

🏃 The Shortcut Analogy

Think of it like paths: The direct path between two points (one side) must always be shorter than going via a third point (sum of other two sides). That's the shortest path principle!
