📏 Step 1: Draw Base Line

Draw a horizontal line using your ruler. This is line 'l' — our reference line.

💡 Why does this work?
Both lines are perpendicular to the ruler edge (90°). Since corresponding angles are equal, the lines must be parallel! This is the mathematical proof behind the construction.
