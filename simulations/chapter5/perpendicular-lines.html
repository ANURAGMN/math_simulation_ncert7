✏️ What are Perpendicular Lines?

Perpendicular lines are a special case of intersecting lines where all four angles are equal and each angle measures exactly 90° (a right angle).

🔲 The Right Angle Symbol

When lines are perpendicular, we mark the angle with a small square (□) instead of an arc. This symbol indicates a 90° angle.
