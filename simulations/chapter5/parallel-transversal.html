🟢 Corresponding Angles

Angles in the same position at each intersection. When lines are parallel, they are equal.

∠1 = ∠5, ∠2 = ∠6, ∠3 = ∠7, ∠4 = ∠8

🔵 Alternate Angles

Angles on opposite sides of the transversal, between the parallel lines. They are equal.

∠3 = ∠6 (Alternate Interior)
∠4 = ∠5 (Alternate Interior)

🟡 Co-Interior Angles

Angles on the same side of transversal, between parallel lines. They add up to 180°.

∠3 + ∠5 = 180°
∠4 + ∠6 = 180°

🔴 Vertically Opposite

Angles opposite each other at an intersection point. Always equal.

∠1 = ∠3, ∠2 = ∠4, ∠5 = ∠7, ∠6 = ∠8
