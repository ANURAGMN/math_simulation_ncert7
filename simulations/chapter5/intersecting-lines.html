🔄 Vertically Opposite Angles

When two lines intersect, the angles opposite to each other are always equal.
∠a = ∠c and ∠b = ∠d

📏 Linear Pairs

Adjacent angles formed by intersecting lines always add up to 180°.
∠a + ∠b = 180°, ∠b + ∠c = 180°
