## TOP 5 KEY CONCEPTS

### Concept 1: Tenths — Splitting a Unit into 10 Equal Parts (Section 3.2)

**What it covers:**
- A unit divided into 10 equal parts gives one-tenth (1/10)
- 3 4/10 can be read as "3 and 4 one-tenths" = 34 one-tenths = 34/10
- Measuring length on a ruler: marks between whole numbers represent tenths
- Adding tenths: 2 7/10 + 3 6/10 = 5 13/10 = 6 3/10 (13 tenths = 1 unit + 3 tenths)
- Subtracting tenths: 12 4/10 − 6 7/10 → borrow 1 unit = 10 tenths → 11 14/10 − 6 7/10

**Simulation potential:** HIGH — Visual ruler/number line where student drags to place tenths, sees how 10 tenths = 1 unit. Animated carrying/borrowing.

---

### Concept 2: Hundredths — Splitting a Tenth into 10 Parts (Section 3.3)

**What it covers:**
- Each one-tenth split into 10 parts gives one-hundredth (1/100)
- 100 hundredths = 1 unit, 10 hundredths = 1 tenth
- Length 4 4/10 5/100 = "4 units, 4 tenths, 5 hundredths" = 445 hundredths
- Adding/subtracting with hundredths: same carry/borrow logic as whole numbers
- Parallel to whole number addition: 483 + 268 uses same regrouping as 15.34 + 2.68

**Simulation potential:** HIGH — Zoom-in number line (unit → tenths → hundredths). Visual parallel between whole-number and decimal addition.

---

### Concept 3: Decimal Place Value & Notation (Section 3.4)

**What it covers:**
- The decimal point ('.') separates whole number part from fractional part
- Place values extend right: units → tenths → hundredths → thousandths (each ÷10)
- 705 vs 70.5 vs 7.05: same digits, completely different values!
- Reading: 0.274 = "zero point two seven four" (NOT "two hundred seventy four")
- Why we use base 10: natural extension of Indian place value system
- Extension: 10,000 → 1000 → 100 → 10 → 1 → 0.1 → 0.01 → 0.001 → 0.0001

**Simulation potential:** EXCELLENT — Interactive place value chart that extends left (thousands) and right (thousandths) with visual ×10/÷10 connections.

---

### Concept 4: Unit Conversions with Decimals (Section 3.5)

**What it covers:**
- mm ↔ cm: 1 mm = 0.1 cm (1/10 cm), 1 cm = 10 mm
- cm ↔ m: 1 cm = 0.01 m (1/100 m), 1 m = 100 cm
- g ↔ kg: 1 g = 0.001 kg (1/1000 kg), 1 kg = 1000 g
- mg ↔ g: 1 mg = 0.001 g
- paise ↔ rupees: 1 paisa = ₹0.01 (1/100 rupee)
- Real-world applications: medication doses (0.05 mg vs 0.5 mg = 10× difference!)

**Simulation potential:** HIGH — Unit conversion calculator with visual scale showing relative sizes. Real-world disaster stories as context.

---

### Concept 5: Comparing, Locating & Ordering Decimals (Section 3.6)

**What it covers:**
- Number line location: zoom in (unit → tenths → hundredths → thousandths) to locate 4.185
- Comparison: digit-by-digit from left, stop at first difference
- The Zero Dilemma: 0.2 = 0.20 = 0.200 (trailing zeros don't matter), but 0.2 ≠ 0.02 ≠ 0.002
- Closest decimal: which of 0.9, 1.01, 1.1, 1.11 is closest to 1? (Answer: 1.01)
- Decimal sequences: 4.4, 4.8, 5.2, 5.6... (add 0.4 each time)
- Deceptive notation: 4.5 hours ≠ 4:50 (it's 4:30), cricket "5.5 overs" = 5 overs 5 balls

**Simulation potential:** EXCELLENT — Zoomable number line, comparison quiz, sequence pattern finder.
