Decimal Arithmetic

Add & subtract decimals step by step

The Key Rule
Adding and subtracting decimals works exactly like whole numbers — line up the decimal points, then add/subtract column by column from right to left. Carry and borrow work the same way: 10 hundredths = 1 tenth, 10 tenths = 1 unit.

How to Use

Key Concept
Always align decimal points! Pad with zeros if needed (18 = 18.0, 5.3 = 5.30). Then add/subtract each column starting from the right. 10 thousandths = 1 hundredth, 10 hundredths = 1 tenth, 10 tenths = 1 unit.
