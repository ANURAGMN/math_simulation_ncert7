Unit Converter

mm, cm, m, g, kg, paise, rupees — all decimals!

Decimal Disasters!
In 2013, Amsterdam sent €188 million instead of €1.8 million — they programmed cents instead of euros. A misplaced decimal point can mean 100× too much! In medicine, reading 0.05 mg as 0.5 mg means 10× the dose. Decimals matter.

How to Use

What To Do
1Pick a conversion type: mm↔cm, cm↔m, g↔kg, or paise↔₹.
2Drag the slider to change the value. Both units update instantly.
3Read the explanation showing the division/multiplication step.
Key Concept
mm→cm: ÷10 (move decimal 1 left). cm→m: ÷100 (move 2 left). g→kg: ÷1000 (move 3 left). paise→₹: ÷100 (move 2 left). It's all about powers of 10!
