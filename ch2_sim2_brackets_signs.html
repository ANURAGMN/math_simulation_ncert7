Brackets & Sign Rules

See what happens when you remove brackets

The Sign Rule
When removing brackets preceded by a minus sign, every term inside flips its sign (+ becomes −, − becomes +). When preceded by a plus sign, signs stay the same.

How to Use This Simulation

Try This
Key example: 200 − (40 + 3). The minus before the bracket flips both signs: +40 becomes −40, +3 becomes −3. Result: 200 − 40 − 3 = 157.
Tricky: 500 − (250 − 100). The −100 inside becomes +100! So: 500 − 250 + 100 = 350. (NOT 500 − 250 − 100 = 150!)
Plus before bracket: 28 + (35 − 10). Nothing flips! Just remove brackets: 28 + 35 − 10 = 53.
Key Concept
Think of it this way: subtracting a sum means subtracting each part. Subtracting a difference means subtracting the first part but adding back the second part (because you subtracted too much!).
