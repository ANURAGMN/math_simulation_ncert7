Distributive Property

Multiple of a sum = Sum of multiples

The Property
Multiplying a number by a sum (or difference) inside brackets equals multiplying by each part separately.
a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c

How to Use This Simulation

Key Concept
The rectangle area a×(b+c) can be split into two smaller rectangles a×b and a×c. Their areas add up to the total. This IS the distributive property — visible as geometry!
